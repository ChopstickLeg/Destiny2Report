# Requested Statistics Summary

## Pullable From API or Derivable

- Total play time.
- PvE / Crucible / Gambit playtime split.
- Patrol time grouped by destination.
- Class playtime split.
- Titles / seals.
- Good Boy Protocol / Archie pet stat.
- Fish caught stat.
- Misadventures.
- Zero-kill activities.
- Overall kill count.
- Approximate non-activity time.
- First raid completion.
- First dungeon completion.
- Day 1 raid completions.
- Day 1 dungeon completions.
- Contest Mode indicator for raid/dungeon PGCRs.
- First raid flawless.
- First dungeon flawless.
- First dungeon solo flawless.
- Most common 3-player fireteam combo.
- Most common 6-player fireteam combo.
- Unique players encountered.
- Inferred first-raid "carry" / first-clear-with-teammate count.
- Crucible K/D and KDA.
- Gambit K/D and KDA.
- Crucible wins and losses.
- Gambit wins and losses.
- Crucible rival / most played against opponent.
- Crucible record against most played opponent.
- K/D in games where the rival opponent was present.
- Gambit motes banked.
- Gambit motes lost.
- Top 10 PvE weapons by weapon kills.
- Top 10 Crucible weapons by weapon kills.
- Top 10 Gambit weapons by weapon kills.

## Not Derivable From API

- Emotes used.
- Total distance ran / traveled.
- Exact orbit time (though non-activity time can be calculated).
- True longest play session.
- True head-to-head K/D against a specific Crucible opponent.
- Exact weapon time-used / time-wielded.
- Retroactive time spent on each subclass.
